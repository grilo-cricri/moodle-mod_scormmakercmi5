# Learner guide

1. Open the activity in your course. You see the status of each unit of the course (not started, in progress,
   completed, passed, failed, waived) and of the whole course.
2. Choose a unit and open it. It opens inside the page (with a Full screen button) or in its own window, depending
   on the course. Closing it from the course returns you to the activity. "Back to the activity" above the unit first
   lets the course save your results, which can take a few seconds.
3. Your progress is saved. Open the course again later to continue where you stopped, with the same registration.
4. If your teacher offers Browse or Review, you can open a unit just to look at it; these modes do not change your
   result.
5. Your grade appears in the course gradebook; if the teacher set activity completion, the activity is marked done
   when you satisfy the course.

Some activities are hosted outside Moodle: the activity page and the player then say so, with the address of that
site. That site receives your Moodle user id (not your name) and records your progress in Moodle.

If the course loads but does not save progress, or the page says your session ended, reload the activity and open the
unit again, and tell your teacher or administrator. Opening a second unit while another is open ends the first one:
use one unit at a time, in one tab.

The Moodle App is not supported for this activity; use a browser.

What is stored about you: see the Privacy section of the README. You can ask your site's privacy officer to export or
delete it.
